# titan-watchface-design

My entries for the **Titan Smart / Fastrack Smart Watch Face Design Challenge 2026** ("Design Time Your Way"), run with Hack2skill. Deadline: **December 6, 2026**.

This repo holds the working material and, later, the watch face designs.

## Files

- `Titan-Watchface-Design-Kit.pdf` - the official design kit (21 pages).
- `Titan-Watchface-GUIDE.txt` - my distilled rules and submission checklist, taken from the kit and the participant handbook. The guide refers to the kit as `original-kit/`; here the PDF sits in the repo root.
- `Titan-Watchface-CONCEPTS.txt` - concept directions I am considering.

## Status

Research and concepts. Designs will be added as they are finished.
