# Titan Watch Face Design Challenge 2026

Deadline: December 6, 2026, 23:59 IST (registration + submission). Results ~Dec 21.
Challenge page: https://hack2skill.com/event/watchfacedesignchallenge2026/
Support: support+titan-WFDC@hack2skill.com

## Canvas and layout
- Baseline canvas: round, 466x466 px (1.43" AMOLED panel on Titan Celestor, Fastrack Astor FR2 Pro, Radiant FX3). No official size is published; treat 466x466 as the safe default and design so it survives cropping.
- Zone A (inner circle): time and data ONLY. Zone B (outer ring): decoration ONLY.
- 30-degree placement grid, centre-aligned composition.
- Time display is MANDATORY. Date/day/weather/battery/steps/heart rate are optional.
- Minimum sizes: text >= 21px, icons >= 25px.
- Colour: 60-30-10 rule (60 dominant / 30 secondary / 10 accent).

## Hard rules (rejection territory)
- NO brand logos anywhere (no Titan, no Fastrack, nothing).
- Only fonts free for commercial use (Google Fonts, Fontshare, freefaces.gallery). Departure Mono (SIL OFL) qualifies.
- Design must be original and unpublished.
- AI may assist ideation/textures/backgrounds only. No mainly-AI-generated designs. Titan can inspect source files and edit history. Disclose AI use in the concept note.
- Third-party assets must be commercially usable, transferable and sublicensable; disclose sources/licences.

## Judging pillars (design for these)
1. Glanceability/ergonomics - readable in under a second, at arm's length, on a tiny round screen.
2. Visual polish/aesthetics - clean typography, balanced spacing, coherent palette.
3. Contextual versatility - works day/night, formal/casual, light and dark wrists.
4. Technical execution - correct file structure, layers, export specs.

## Figma submission spec (his track)
- Editable Figma file, share link with Can-Edit permission.
- Named frames per state: Active / AOD (always-on display) / Interactive.
- Organized, named layers.
- PNG exports at 2x and 3x.
- Concept note: 2-3 sentences (Adobe track: 50-100 words).
- Up to 10 Figma entries (+ 10 Adobe = 20 max). 2 attempts per entry; latest version is evaluated.
- Submit via dashboard: Submissions > Ongoing Submission > Project Submissions > Titan Watch Face Design Challenge 2026.

## Ownership (T&C)
Every submission irrevocably assigns ALL rights to Titan, win or lose, no royalties. Only submit designs made fresh for this challenge; never anything Nadeem wants to keep or reuse. Portfolio display allowed only after results, and not for shortlisted designs.

## His taste (for concepting)
Tony Stark / JARVIS holographic HUD, terminal-like, dark backgrounds, functional fun. Likes Departure Mono. Dislikes chrome/metallic looks and generic polished templates. Titan = premium Indian watch brand; Fastrack = youth brand. See CONCEPTS.md for starting directions.
